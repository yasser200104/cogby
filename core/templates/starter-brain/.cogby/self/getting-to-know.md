# Getting to know you

A few questions Cogby asks over time: never more than one in a chat, and only at a natural pause.
Each is asked once and can be skipped. A ticked line says when it was asked and what you said.

- [ ] What matters most to you right now?
- [ ] What do you already use Claude for, and what do you wish it did for you every day?
- [ ] Where do you keep your to-dos?
- [ ] Are your meetings recorded or written up somewhere?
- [ ] Want a morning check-in?
